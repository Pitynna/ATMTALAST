As the Warden, you can implant criminals you suspect will reoffend with devices that track their location and let you remotely inject them with incapacitating chemicals.
As the Warden, keep a constant eye on the armory, as it is a favorite target of nuclear operatives and bold traitors.

As the Chief Engineer, you can rename areas or create entirely new ones using the station blueprints.

As the Head of Personnel, you are as big a target as the Captain because of the potential power your ID card and computer can hand out.
As the Head of Security, you must coordinate your department to deal with any threat that arises on the station. Sometimes that means using the armory against a blob, sometimes being ruthless during a vampire or cult invasion.
As the Head of Security, don't let power go to your head. You may have high access, great gear and a miniature army on your side, but being a terrible person for no good reason is grounds for a ban.
