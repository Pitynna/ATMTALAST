# Wall-mounted disposal sprites

`disposal_wall.dmi` contains the eight `disposal` states extracted from
Goonstation's `icons/obj/disposal_small.dmi`, retrieved on 2026-11-05.
All four directions and original frame pixels are preserved. The surplus ninth
delay entry in the upstream eight-frame flush animation is omitted.
